OSRS cache folder
=================

You only need this if you have NO game server folder to point the Studio at.

With a game server, the Studio reads the server's own cache (its caches\ folder) and this folder stays empty.

Without one, put an OSRS cache here and the Studio runs by itself: every viewer and editor works, and "Export
files" writes what you make into the Studio's export folder.

What a cache looks like
  The game's own cache files:

      osrs-cache\
          main_file_cache.dat2
          main_file_cache.idx0
          main_file_cache.idx1  ...
          main_file_cache.idx255

  They can sit directly in here, or one or two folders down.

Where to get one
  Caches are not included: the game data belongs to Jagex.
  The OpenRS2 Archive (https://archive.openrs2.org) lists Old School caches. Take the "Cache (.dat2/.idx)"
  download called disk.zip and unpack it here.

You can also keep the cache anywhere else: press "Game folder" at the top of the app and choose where it is.
